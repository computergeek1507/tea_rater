# Privacy Policy for Tea Rater

_Last updated: 2026-07-26_

Tea Rater is a personal tea-tracking app. This policy explains what data the
app accesses and how it's used.

## Data collection

Tea Rater does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any
server. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising.

All tea entries — names, ratings, notes, and brew counts — are stored only
in local app storage on your device. This data is never sent anywhere. It
stays on your device unless you explicitly export it yourself (see below).

## Camera permission

Tea Rater can use your device's camera to scan text off a tea package label,
to help you fill in the tea's name. This is done entirely on-device using
Android's ML Kit text recognition. No image or video is ever uploaded,
stored, or shared — camera frames are processed locally in memory and
discarded immediately after recognition.

## File export / import

Tea Rater lets you export your tea list to a JSON file, and import a
previously exported file. This is a manual, user-initiated action using your
device's file picker. Exported files are saved wherever you choose on your
device or its connected storage — Tea Rater does not upload them anywhere.

## Third-party services

Tea Rater does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or
tracking SDKs.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same
location with a revised "Last updated" date.
