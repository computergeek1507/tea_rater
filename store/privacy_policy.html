Privacy Policy

For the Tea Rater Android app

Last updated 26 Jul 2026

Tea Rater is a personal tea-tracking app built for one job: keeping a tasting log of the teas you try. This page explains what data the app touches, in full, and why.

Data collection

Tea Rater does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising.

Every tea entry — name, rating, notes, brew count — is stored only in local app storage on your device. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it yourself, as described below.

Camera permission

Tea Rater can use your device's camera to scan text off a tea package, so you can fill in a tea's name without typing it. This runs entirely on-device using Android's ML Kit text recognition. No image or video is ever uploaded, stored, or shared — each camera frame is processed in memory and discarded immediately after recognition.

File export & import

You can export your tea list to a JSON file, and import a previously exported file back in. This is a manual, user-initiated action using your device's file picker. Exported files go wherever you choose on your device or its connected storage — Tea Rater does not upload them anywhere.

Third-party services

Tea Rater does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same location with a revised "last updated" date.
