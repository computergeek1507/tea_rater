# Play Store listing — Tea Rater

## Title (28/30 chars)
Tea Rater: Track & Rate Teas

## Short description (77/80 chars)
Rate teas thumbs up or down, track brews, and scan labels with your camera.

## Full description
Keep a tasting log of every tea you brew.

Tea Rater is a simple, no-frills way to track the teas you try and remember
which ones are worth reaching for again.

FEATURES
• Rate each tea thumbs up, thumbs down, or in between
• Jot down notes on flavor, steep time, or where you got it
• Tap the counter to track how many times you've brewed a tea
• Search your collection by name or notes
• Scan a tea's name straight off the package with your camera (Android,
  on-device — nothing is uploaded)
• Back up or transfer your list anytime with JSON export/import

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your tea list stays on your device.
